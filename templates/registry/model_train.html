Train a model

Upload historical data and the platform trains a classifier on it, reports baseline accuracy, precision and recall on a held-out portion, and registers the result as a new version.

The training split also becomes the baseline that monitoring compares against. A model has to be judged against the data it was actually trained on, so both come from this one upload.
