How a feature gets its status

Magnitude decides the band — the worse of PSI and Jensen-Shannon. The statistical test (K-S for numeric, Chi-Square for categorical) only confirms it. A large magnitude the test cannot confirm is downgraded one level, because on a big batch a trivial shift will look significant, and on a small one a real shift will not.
