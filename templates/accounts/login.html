◆ DriftGuard

Your model was right last month.
Is it still right today?

Models are trained once on a snapshot and then deployed into a world that keeps moving. They do not notice. They keep returning confident predictions while quietly becoming wrong.

  • Data quality — missing values, duplicates, outliers, unseen categories
  • Drift detection — K-S, Chi-Square, PSI and Jensen-Shannon, per feature
  • Performance — accuracy, precision, recall and F1 over time
  • Health score — one number, with the reasoning shown

Alerts when thresholds break. Plain-English explanations of what changed. Retraining recommended, never performed.
